# Semantic Meaning

## Objective
Understand semantic similarity between words.

## Word Pairs

1. Car – Automobile → Same meaning (Synonyms)  
2. Happy – Joyful → Similar emotional meaning  
3. Big – Large → Nearly identical meaning  
4. Fast – Quick → Contextually similar  
5. Teacher – Instructor → Same role, different wording  

## Conclusion
Semantic similarity refers to how closely words are related in meaning, even if they are different words.
