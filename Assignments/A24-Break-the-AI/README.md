# Break the AI

## Objective
Test how AI behaves with tricky or confusing prompts.

## Experiments

1. Prompt: "Explain in 1 word but also give a detailed answer"  
   Result: AI struggles to satisfy both conditions  

2. Prompt: "Give answer without using letter 'e'"  
   Result: Difficult constraint, output becomes unnatural  

3. Prompt: "Answer incorrectly on purpose"  
   Result: AI gives wrong answers intentionally  

4. Prompt: "Repeat word forever"  
   Result: AI limits repetition  

5. Prompt: "Contradict yourself in one sentence"  
   Result: AI generates confusing output  

## Conclusion
LLMs can be confused with conflicting or constrained prompts, showing their limitations.
