# Prompt Engineering

## Objective
Compare weak vs strong prompts for different tasks.

---

## 1. Resume

Weak Prompt:
"Write a resume"

Strong Prompt:
"Write a professional resume for a computer science student with skills in Python, ML, and internships"

---

## 2. Business Idea

Weak Prompt:
"Give business idea"

Strong Prompt:
"Suggest a scalable AI-based business idea for students with low investment"

---

## 3. Study Plan

Weak Prompt:
"Make study plan"

Strong Prompt:
"Create a 2-month study plan for learning Machine Learning with daily 2-hour schedule"

---

## Conclusion
Strong prompts give:
- Clear instructions  
- Better outputs  
- More accurate results
